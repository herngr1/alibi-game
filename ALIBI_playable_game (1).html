ALIBI
Everyone has an alibi. Everyone has a secret.

A pass-the-phone game of bluffing, secrets & deduction for 2–8 players.

How to Play

1. A crime is revealed. Each player secretly receives a role, secret, clue and personal objective.

2. Take turns interrogating one another. You may tell the truth, lie, use half-truths, or redirect suspicion.

3. Each player gets 2 CALL BLUFF tokens. Challenge a factual claim when you think someone is lying. A correct challenge earns Evidence; a bad challenge rewards the accused.

4. New evidence appears as rounds pass. After the interrogation, everyone secretly votes.

5. Catch the culprit to win as an investigator. The culprit wins by escaping the final vote. Secret objectives earn bragging-right bonus points.

Important: innocent players have secrets and objectives too. A lie does not automatically mean guilt.
